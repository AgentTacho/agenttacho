# Accounts and budgets

Each entry in the bar is an icon and a percentage. Click it for the details. The dropdown has one section per account or group, set apart by alternating background bands (plain, then faintly tinted), and a footer on its own band pinned below them with any source errors, the update line and one status row: the refresh icon with "Last refreshed" beside it on the left, Settings and Quit on the right.

- **Subscription accounts** (Claude Code on a Claude Pro or Max plan) show the plan windows Anthropic reports (today the 7-day window) and the model limits such as the one for Fable, each with its reset time and a bar with the pace tick. Only the windows in Anthropic's answer get a row: a window Anthropic stops reporting disappears, and a new or returning one shows up without an AgentTacho update. A window of a kind AgentTacho does not know the length of has no pace tick. The bar shows the 7-day window. In the expanded Details, the `Σ` column at the end of the day row holds the tokens used this week. If a refresh cannot reach Anthropic, for example right after waking from sleep, the values stay as they are and AgentTacho tries once more 30 seconds later; only when that fails too are they marked stale (`~`), with the time of the last good values. If Anthropic rejects the sign-in, the values are marked stale and the dropdown says in red to run `claude` to sign in again. The last good values are saved, so after a relaunch they are back right away, even without a network, marked stale until the next successful refresh.
- **Token-based accounts** (Google Vertex AI or the OpenAI API) show spend in USD, computed from the local usage data of Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode with a built-in price table you can override, measured against a weekly or monthly budget you set. An account has its own budget or belongs to a budget group. The amount of an own budget is optional: without one the account has no limit and shows its real spend or its tokens, without a percent or menu bar entry, and models without a price can be valued at a reference model's prices (see [Configuration](configuration.md#accounts-without-a-limit)). Each account's **Dropdown shows** setting (`show` in the configuration file) picks whether its dropdown headline shows the percent, the tokens or the money. A No limit account of an earlier build loads as an own budget without an amount, and its Price tokens as model (formerly Value as) now values only the records of models without a price (see [Configuration](configuration.md#accounts-without-a-limit)). Each token-based account reads one harness, Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode, and its match rule says which of that harness's usage is its own (see [Configuration](configuration.md#matching-usage-to-accounts)). For one Vertex budget across Claude Code and OpenCode, add a Claude Code account and an OpenCode account, both with the Vertex preset, and put both into one budget group.
- **Codex subscription accounts** (Codex on a ChatGPT plan) show the plan's limits: every window of the plan (for example 5-hour and weekly on Plus, 30-day on Free) with its percent and reset time. The bar shows the longest window. The limits come live from the ChatGPT usage service, so usage on other machines and in the cloud counts too. When the service cannot be reached, AgentTacho tries once more 30 seconds later before marking anything stale. If that fails too, or the Codex sign-in has expired, the newer of the last live limits and the last limits Codex recorded on this Mac is shown, marked stale. The last live limits are saved, so they survive a relaunch. A rejected sign-in is noted in red. The expanded Details show the same breakdown for the tokens used through the plan this week, with the week's total in the `Σ` column of the day row.
- **Budget groups** put token-based accounts under one shared budget, either weekly or monthly. The group's amount is optional: a group without one has no limit and just adds up its members' spend or tokens in one dropdown section, with no percent, bar or menu bar entry, which suits free models used from several harnesses (see [Configuration](configuration.md#groups-without-a-limit)). Like an account, each group has **Price tokens as** and **Dropdown shows** settings (`valueAs` and `show`). A weekly budget lines up with your subscription's 7-day reset, or with a weekday and hour you choose. A monthly budget runs from midnight on the 1st to the end of the calendar month in local time, which suits a company spending limit granted per month. You add and remove groups in the Groups tab. A group can be empty: it stays when its last account moves elsewhere or is removed, shows 0% (or "0" without an amount), and keeps its settings and its place in the bar until an account joins it again. In the dropdown, a group with two or more members lists each member with its spend and share under the group's bar (without an amount, its spend or tokens and no share), and has a single Details disclosure for the whole budget. Its day row ends with the period's spend in the `Σ` column. When two or more members have usage, the Details end with a "Show account details" button that adds each member's own tables and day row below the group's; "Hide account details" folds them away again, and the choice applies to every group.
- **Member shares.** A group member can also appear in the bar on its own, showing its spend as a percent of the group budget. While the group has no amount there is no share: the member's checkbox is disabled and its setting is kept for when an amount is entered. It sits right after its group, or in the group's place when the group itself is hidden.
